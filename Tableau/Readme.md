# Tableau Dashboard

## Overview

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive visualization of marketing campaign performance across channels, campaigns, and time periods. It summarizes the key KPIs generated during the SQL and Python analysis, enabling business users to monitor performance and identify trends without writing queries or code.

---

# Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive visualizations for:

- Revenue Overview
- Marketing Spend Analysis
- ROAS by Marketing Channel
- Revenue by Marketing Channel
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Campaign Performance
- CTR and Conversion Analysis
- Geographic Performance
- KPI Summary Cards
- Interactive Filters (Channel, Campaign Type, Date, Weather, Holiday)

---

# Business Questions Answered

- Which marketing channels generate the highest revenue?
- Which channels deliver the highest ROAS?
- How has revenue changed over time?
- Which campaigns perform best?
- How do external factors affect campaign performance?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?

# Key Insights

- Google generates the highest overall revenue.
- Email delivers the highest ROAS.
- Conversions have a stronger impact on revenue than impressions alone.
- Holiday campaigns generally outperform non-holiday campaigns.
- Campaign performance varies across channels and time periods.
