### Insight 1: Marketing Channel significantly influences Revenue

Statistical hypothesis testing (ANOVA) confirmed that marketing channels have a significant impact on campaign revenue. Among all channels, **Google, Facebook, and TV** contributed the highest revenue uplift in the predictive models.

---

### Insight 2: Conversions drive Revenue more than Clicks

The Linear Regression model identified **Conversions** as one of the strongest numerical predictors of revenue. Increasing conversions has a substantially greater business impact than simply generating additional clicks.

---

### Insight 3: Holidays improve Campaign Performance

Independent t-test results showed that campaigns executed during **holidays** generate significantly different revenue compared to non-holiday campaigns, indicating that seasonal marketing positively influences business outcomes.

---

### Insight 4: Weather affects Marketing Effectiveness

ANOVA testing demonstrated that **weather conditions significantly influence revenue**. Campaign performance tends to vary under different weather conditions, suggesting that external factors should be considered during campaign planning.

---

### Insight 5: Discount Percentage impacts Revenue

Statistical testing confirmed that different discount levels produce significantly different revenue outcomes. However, the predictive model indicates that discount percentage has a relatively smaller influence than marketing channel selection and conversions.

---

### Insight 6: Random Forest provides the most accurate Revenue Predictions

Among the predictive models developed, **Random Forest Regression achieved the highest predictive accuracy (R² = 91.67%)**, outperforming Multiple Linear Regression (R² = 87.32%) while capturing nonlinear relationships in marketing data.

---

### Insight 7: Marketing effectiveness is driven by quality rather than spend alone

Although marketing spend contributes positively to revenue, the analysis shows that **campaign effectiveness depends more on channel selection, audience engagement, and conversion performance** than simply increasing advertising budgets.
