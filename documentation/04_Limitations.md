
Although the project provides valuable business insights and accurate revenue predictions, several limitations should be considered:

### 1. Synthetic Dataset

- The analysis is based on a synthetically generated dataset designed to simulate real-world marketing campaigns.
    
- Actual business data may contain additional complexities, noise, and unforeseen patterns.


### 2. Limited Business Variables

- The model includes campaign, customer, channel, weather, holiday, and discount information.
    
- Other factors such as customer lifetime value, pricing strategy, competitor promotions, and macroeconomic conditions were not included.


### 3. Model Scope

- Linear Regression and Random Forest Regression were used for revenue prediction.
    
- More advanced Marketing Mix Modeling techniques such as XGBoost, LightGBM, Bayesian MMM, and Adstock modeling could further improve forecasting accuracy.


### 4. Generalization

- The predictive models were trained and evaluated on the available dataset.
    
- Model performance should be revalidated when applied to new campaigns or real-world marketing environments.
