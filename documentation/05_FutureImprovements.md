
The MartPulse AI project can be extended further through the following enhancements:

### 1. Advanced Marketing Mix Modeling

- Implement Adstock transformations to capture delayed advertising effects.
    
- Incorporate saturation curves to model diminishing returns from increased marketing spend.
    

### 2. Enhanced Feature Engineering

- Include additional business variables such as pricing changes, customer lifetime value (CLV), promotional campaigns, competitor strategies, and macroeconomic indicators.
    

### 3. Advanced Machine Learning Models

- Evaluate models such as XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, and Bayesian Marketing Mix Models to further improve prediction accuracy and model robustness.
    

### 4. Marketing Budget Optimization

- Develop an optimization engine that recommends the optimal budget allocation across marketing channels based on expected revenue and ROI.
    

### 5. Real-Time Analytics

- Integrate the solution with cloud data pipelines and automated dashboard refreshes to support real-time campaign monitoring and business decision-making.
