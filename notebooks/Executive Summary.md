
- Analyzed **500,000 marketing campaign records** to identify the key drivers of campaign revenue.
    
- Estimated the **average campaign revenue** using **95% Confidence Intervals**.
    
- Performed **ANOVA** and **Independent t-tests** to assess the impact of discounts, weather, marketing channels, weekends, and holidays on revenue.
    
- Built and evaluated **Multiple Linear Regression** and **Random Forest Regression** models for campaign revenue prediction.
    
- **Random Forest** achieved the best predictive performance (**Testing R² = 91.67%**), outperforming Multiple Linear Regression.
    
- Identified **Google campaigns, Facebook campaigns, conversions, and marketing spend** as the most influential factors affecting revenue.
    
- Recommended prioritizing high-performing channels, optimizing conversion rates, and using **Random Forest** for campaign revenue forecasting.
